A POS system for tracking sales, revenue, and other financial data for a coffee shop franchise.

The Project requirements were simple: Make a POS System that will allow the cashier to be able to instantly collect the order of the customer, and those data will be sent to the owner for later analysis, including daily, weekly, and monthly sales. The system also includes an inventory tracking that takes notes of increases or decreases in stocks for both stockrooms and storefront systems.
