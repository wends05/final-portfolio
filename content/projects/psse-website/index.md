The Software Engineering department needed its own visibility to the public, and there is no website that was made from before. Additionally, the PSSE (Philippine Society of Software Engineers) also wanted a place to promote the organization aside from its [Facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/the.psse/), namely the events, officers, and the projects.

The approach used here is similar to how we practice things in Software Engineering: Gather requirements, gather as a team to plan the sprint, do independent development, and do DSMs to address blockers and see progress, and a recap and reflection at the end. In other words, practicing scrum to ensure requirements are met while being able to address changes in the system.

The result is a public site showing the department's curriculum, events, projects, and officers, plus a management system for the officers and faculty can update the work of the organization.
