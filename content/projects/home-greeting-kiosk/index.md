A small project for the completion of one of my academic requirements on my machine learning subject. Goal was to be able to make a machine learning model, use that, and create a simple interface with that model.

A simple project was enough so me and my pair made a sort of greeting kiosk to identify who has arrived at the door, then greet them and display their name on the screen on the connected ESP32.
