One of the most important skills to learn, as it is the foundation for building interactive and user-friendly applications. Just like backend development, frontend development is one of the things I learned during university.

Initiially thought that front-end was purely design, but later it turns out it's also improving user experience for the application: fetching data from the backend, knowing about loading and error states, proper react component composition and patterns, state management, learning about the DOM, etc.
